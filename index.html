How it works

Modeling notes

This project mixes published factory specs with conservative engineering estimates. Tune gains are engine-family aware; supporting hardware unlocks higher tune/fuel potential instead of simply stacking arbitrary horsepower. The drag model numerically integrates tractive force versus aerodynamic and rolling losses through the quarter mile.

The catalog now spans the main production trim families and body variants across the E, F and G generations of Series 2–8. Because BMW outputs, emissions packages, transmissions and curb weights differ by country and model year, entries are representative rather than VIN-specific.

Use the drag strip as a simulation only. Real acceleration testing belongs at a legal closed course with proper safety equipment and supervision.

Sources & image policy

BMW Group PressClub photography

V5 only displays manually curated BMW press/professional vehicle images. Random web-image fallback was removed so people, watermarks, screenshots and cluttered owner photos do not appear unexpectedly.

Exterior reference catalogs

ADRO, Vorsteiner, BMW M Performance, 3D Design, Maxton Design and other BMW-focused brands are represented as visual/fitment references where applicable.

Supporting-hardware references

Public VRSF, CSF, Wagner, ARM and similar catalog information is used for non-installation product reference cards and price snapshots.

BMW / BMW Group press material

Factory engine outputs, model positioning and new-model technical figures.
